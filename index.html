Maker Space 3D Print Recommendations

  • • Multi-Material / Multi-Tool: Download .3MF. Pre-split dual bodies for multi-tool or multi-filament printers (Prusa MMU, Bambu AMS, Voron, Toolchanger) or slicer color-change pauses (M600).
  • • Single-Color / Universal: Download .STL for single-extruder prints on any printer in the Maker Space (Prusa MK3/MK4, Ultimaker, Creality, etc.).
  • • Orientation: Print flat on backplate (0° supports required).
  • • Layer Height: 0.20 mm standard (0.16 mm for ultra-crisp corner emblem definition).
  • • Walls / Infill: 3 perimeters (solid lanyard loop) • 15–20% Gyroid or Grid.
  • • Print Time & Material: ~25 min | ~10g PLA / PETG (featherweight).
